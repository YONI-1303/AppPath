LOCAL CHECKLIST FOR MILESTONE 3

1. Open AppPath with `npm start`.
2. Run a scan on an APK you are authorized to inspect.
3. In Analyze APK, try the finding search field, severity filter, and category filter.
4. Click a finding title and confirm the modal shows the real evidence and recommendation.
5. Open Security graph and click a finding node.
6. Open Candidate attack paths and confirm every listed step refers to a finding from the report.
7. Open MASVS checklist and confirm it says partial topic mapping / not certification.
8. In Reports, export JSON, CSV, and SARIF and open each file in a text editor.
9. Close and reopen AppPath, then confirm the latest report is restored.
10. Optional: install/start Ollama, pull qwen2.5-coder:1.5b, refresh AI Fix, and request remediation for one selected finding.
11. Optional: select the official JADX CLI launcher and test code mapping on a small APK.
12. Optional: scan a distinct rebuilt APK hash and compare it in Validation.

If a test fails, keep the terminal output; it helps identify the layer that failed.
